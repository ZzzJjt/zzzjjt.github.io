03 / FOUR-SKILL EVALUATION

Four-skill simulation evaluation

Each fixed command runs continuously for 60 seconds, plus a 70-second command-switching test. Three evaluation seeds (123 / 456 / 789), with 32 environments each: every task completed 96/96 trials without falling.

04 / SIM2SIM → SIM2REAL

Sim2Real starts with the interface

Real G1 EDU+ telemetry reports mode_machine = 6, with the waist roll / pitch slots inactive. Stage 2 builds a locked-waist candidate from the official model family, fixes the fingers while retaining their modeled mass, and retrains a policy for 27 body joints.

One input / output contract

Actions follow SDK indices 0–12 and 15–28. Five frames are stacked per observation term: 90 values become a 450-dimensional Actor input. The Actor does not require measured linear velocity and generates joint targets at 50 Hz. ONNX export and both simulators share the interface definition.

Measuring motion over a control interval

The v2 tracking reward uses displacement and heading change over the full 20 ms control interval, while retaining instantaneous velocity for comparison. This phase runs 600 iterations from v1 model_100. The earlier 37-action policy is not applied directly to hardware.

Fixed tasks last 60 seconds and switching lasts 70 seconds; velocity statistics exclude the first 2 seconds. Translation retains heading feedback. Nominal MuJoCo trials vary only small initial heading offsets. Avoiding falls does not establish tracking accuracy.

The transfer gap is still visible

Nominal forward speed is approximately 0.467 m/s in Isaac and 0.449 m/s in MuJoCo against a 0.5 m/s target (pose-difference measurement in Isaac, state velocity in MuJoCo). MuJoCo standing still drifts about 16.1° in heading. Combined perturbations change friction, PD gains, mass / inertia, and add a 20 ms action delay plus observation noise. Robustness validation is not yet complete.

Real data, read-only inference

An early v2 model_100 candidate processed 250 frames of real LowState telemetry, connecting observation construction to candidate action computation without publishing policy joint targets. This does not validate the final model_599 in physical control.

Next steps

Investigate standing yaw drift and dynamics mismatch; extend training with gain and delay randomization. Verify physical joint directions, PD parameters, IMU coordinates, and hand configuration. Resume physical motion validation when space and prerequisite checks are ready.
